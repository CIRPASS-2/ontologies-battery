Battery lifecycle statuses per Annex XIII 4(c)ni back to ToC or Named Individual ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp/battery-statuses#BatteryStatusScheme

Is defined by
https://w3id.org/eudpp/battery-statuses
belongs to
Concept Scheme c
has facts
references ap "Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, Annex XIII 4(c); BatteryPass-Ready Data Attribute Long List v1.3, attribute 20"@en
has Top Concept ap Original ni
has Top Concept ap Re-used ni
has Top Concept ap Remanufactured ni
has Top Concept ap Repurposed ni
has Top Concept ap Waste ni
pref Label ap "Battery lifecycle statuses per Annex XIII 4(c)"@en
scope Note ap "Closed list of five terms. The status is dynamic at individual-battery granularity: it is updated on placing on the market and on every status change. A new battery passport is issued when the battery has been remanufactured, repurposed or subjected to a treatment operation preparing for re-use or repurposing and is placed on the market again; the link back to the previous passport is carried by the CORE property dpp:linkToPreviousDPP, not by this vocabulary. Access to the battery status is restricted to persons with a legitimate interest — a conformance rule expressed in the SHACL shapes, not in this ontology."@en
