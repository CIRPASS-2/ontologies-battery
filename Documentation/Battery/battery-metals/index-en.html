EU DPP Battery Metals Vocabulary: Description back to ToC

Developed against the CIRPASS-2 CORE ontology v2.0.5 (refresh of 25 August 2026; P_DPP v2.0.6 of 27 August 2026, MAT v1.0.2). To be revisited when MAT v2 lands (ontologies-core#24, #25): #25 introduces eudpp:materialTypeScheme, to be added to these four concepts as it is to the ten chemistries, and recycledContentType, which splits pre- from post-consumer shares.

Battery metals subject to recycled content declaration, Art. 8(1)ni back to ToC or Named Individual ToC

IRI: https://w3id.org/eudpp/battery-metals#BatteryMetalScheme

Is defined by
https://w3id.org/eudpp/battery-metals
belongs to
Concept Scheme c
has facts
references ap "Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, Art. 8(1)"@en
has Top Concept ap Cobalt ni
has Top Concept ap Lead ni
has Top Concept ap Lithium ni
has Top Concept ap Nickel ni
pref Label ap "Battery metals subject to recycled content declaration, Art. 8(1)"@en
scope Note ap "Art. 8(1) does not treat the four metals alike. For cobalt, lithium and nickel it asks for the share present in ACTIVE MATERIALS and recovered from battery manufacturing waste or post-consumer waste; for lead it asks for the share present in THE BATTERY and recovered from waste. The distinction is recorded on each concept and is not expressible in the CORE today."@en
